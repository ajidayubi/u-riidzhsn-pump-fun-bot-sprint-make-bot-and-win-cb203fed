Paper-trading bot for pump.fun bonding curves: buys early momentum, exits on take-profit, stop-loss or trailing fall.

The bot buys steady grinders: a coin that climbed 30–100% from its creation price over 20–100 trades, with at least 16 of its last 20 trades buys, still within 3% of its peak, on a buy trade right now. It risks at most 0.10 SOL per coin, holds at most 5 coins at once, and exits on a plain plan: take profit at +50%, stop loss at −10%, or a 3000-slot time exit. All decisions use integer math on the replay state only — no clock, no randomness, no network — so two runs agree byte for byte.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```
